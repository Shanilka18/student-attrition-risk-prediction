## 1. Audit Overview & Objectives

This audit provides a rigorous methodological and psychometric review of items **Q23 through Q35** from `EnvFin_Questionnaire_v1.md`. Every item is evaluated for:
1. **Measurement Scale & Levels:** Classification as Ordinal, Nominal, or Binary, and exact response scale definition.
2. **Missingness & Structural Non-Applicability:** Distinction between missing data and structural non-applicability (e.g., fully online students legitimately having 0 commute burden).
3. **Sensitive Item Governance:** Treatment of privacy-sensitive socio-economic disclosures (household income, family dependency, cost difficulties) and handling of "Prefer not to say".
4. **Cross-Item Redundancy & Interaction:** Feature correlation, confounding pathways, and role within derived indices.
5. **A-priori Attrition Hypothesis:** Theoretical mechanism linking the environmental/financial stressor to student departure or deferral *(with explicit placeholder: citation to be added by author)*.

## 3. Methodological Synthesis & Recommendations

1. **Structural "Not Applicable" Handling (Q27, Q28, Q29):**
   - Must **never** be coded as missing (NaN) or dropped via listwise deletion.
   - For students studying fully online, `commute_time`, `commute_spend`, and `transport_mode` are structurally nonexistent; they are encoded as explicit zero-pressure states (`0.0` on continuous normalized scale; `-2` in integer storage with dedicated boolean flag `is_online_student=1`).
2. **Informative Missingness / "Prefer Not to Say" (Q26, Q23):**
   - Refusal to disclose financial distress is non-random (MNAR). Dropping these cases introduces severe selection bias.
   - Retain a binary missingness indicator column (`cost_difficulty_pns=1`) and impute the baseline value with median matching strata.
3. **Cross-Component Isolation & Anti-Leakage Guardrail:**
   - Downstream academic consequences (e.g. GPA decline, attendance drop) are consequences of these stressors and must remain quarantined as mediators.
   - Survey items Q23–Q35 strictly capture external environmental and financial constraints prior to academic performance collapse.
