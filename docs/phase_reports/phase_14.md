## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 14 built the non-technical data storytelling layer of the MEI platform across three core routes:
1. **Executive Overview (`/overview`)**: Plain-language framing of the 0–100 Engagement Index, 4 stat cards, an interactive 4-stage horizontal pipeline diagram with empirical cohort counts, a 10-point score distribution histogram with risk band shading, holdout risk band verification table, operational shortcuts, and best-in-category benchmark links.
2. **Study Patterns (`/patterns`)**: 4 pattern cards (Consistent, Sporadic, Declining, Improving) with longitudinal P1–P4 sparklines and 95% CIs, an interactive composite engagement trajectory chart with toggleable $\pm 1$ SD bands, Hidden Markov Model (HMM) latent state emission profiles (7 indicators) and transition dynamics heatmap with expected dwell times, a multi-method clustering comparison table, a pattern $\times$ level cross-matrix, and an honest ablation benchmark showing the standalone and incremental value of trajectory states.
3. **Risk Drivers (`/drivers`)**: Top 10 factors horizontal bar chart with 4-way grouping switches (By Factor, By Question, By Part of Semester, By Type), a non-linear dependence view with scatter jitter and empirical trendlines, a friendly cohort beeswarm plot, pattern small multiples with sample size warnings, an explanation stability audit across 15 CV folds, and an explicit interpretation caution distinguishing statistical association from causality.

All narratives and interpretations are deterministic, generated by pure functions in `frontend/src/content/sentences.ts` (tested with Vitest) and fed directly by active run artifacts without hardcoded metrics or hallucinated copy.

## 5. Decisions & Risks Logged

- **D-058**: Logged in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Established deterministic sentence templating in `sentences.ts` to ensure non-technical university stakeholders receive clear, mathematically grounded explanations without runtime hallucinations or overpromising.
- **Risk Logged (Kernel Surrogate Consistency on Distance-Based Models)**: Non-parametric k-NN models exhibit lower cross-fold feature rank agreement under Kernel surrogate sampling ($\rho \approx 0.08$) compared to tree ensembles ($\rho \approx 0.88$). This is explicitly disclosed on `/drivers` under the stability audit section so staff understand the distinction between tree and instance-based explainers.
- **Scope Boundary Strictly Respected**: The student cohort scoring page (`/score`) was NOT built in this phase and remains reserved for Phase 15.
