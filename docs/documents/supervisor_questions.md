# Questions for Supervisors

1. **Outcome validity:** Is a shared self-reported intention score an acceptable label for this study, given that it measures stated intention rather than actual withdrawal? Should `attrition_intent_score` remain continuous on 0–100 with `at_risk = 1` at 60 or above?
2. **Income sensitivity:** Should the household-income item include an explicit “Prefer not to say” option or allow skipping? How should non-response be represented and reported without pressuring participants or misclassifying it as an income band?
3. **Sample size:** Is the proposal's working minimum of 300 eligible responses adequate for the expected at-risk class and four components? What feasibility or precision criteria should govern the final sample target and subgroup analyses?
4. **Shared outcome and form:** Which exact shared intention items, scoring rule, attention check, and failure coding should all four components use? Who will approve and freeze the common wording and section order before the form is released?
5. **Fairness reporting:** Which participant groups and error/performance measures are appropriate for the group-wise fairness report, and what minimum cell-size suppression rule should apply to every component and website view?
6. **Data storage:** Which institution-approved location, access roles, retention/deletion period, and transfer method should be used for pseudonymous responses and any linkage information? Who is authorised to export the master form responses?
7. **Success while real data are pending:** What should count as acceptable interim progress while responses are being generated—for example, validated questionnaire handling, leakage-free validation, reproducible reporting, and clear separation of synthetic pipeline checks from real findings?
8. **Real-data timeline:** What approvals and milestones must be completed for form launch, recruitment, data freeze, and final real-data analysis, and when should each be expected?
